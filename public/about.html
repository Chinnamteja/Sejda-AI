About Sejda

Helping individuals, legal teams, educators, and global businesses manipulate, sign, and organize documents with speed and complete confidentiality.

Our Mission & Purpose

Founded with the belief that document processing should never require expensive desktop software suites or invasive data harvesting, Sejda provides over 30+ browser-based and desktop PDF utilities. Whether you are a law student merging case briefs, an accountant compressing tax audits under 1 MB, or an executive reviewing contract indemnities with AI, Sejda delivers instantaneous, reliable document tools with zero barrier to entry.

Our Core Values

  • Privacy by Architecture: We never inspect, sell, or use your uploaded documents to train machine learning models. Ephemeral 2-hour automated purge ensures your private files leave no footprint.
  • Open Accessibility: Every visitor enjoys up to 3 free tasks per hour for files up to 200 pages, with no mandatory credit card registration.
  • Speed & Reliability: In-browser WebAssembly processing executes tasks locally without bandwidth lag whenever feasible.
