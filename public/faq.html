🔒 Security, Privacy & Data Purging

Is Sejda safe and private? What happens to my uploaded files?

Your documents remain 100% strictly confidential and your private property. Files uploaded for server processing are held exclusively in volatile RAM and permanently erased after exactly 2 hours via an automated cryptographic purge daemon.

When using our browser-first tools (like offline signing, client-side viewing, and desktop app execution), your files never leave your device at all. We never inspect, share, sell, or train machine learning foundation models on user files.

How does the 2-hour automatic purge guarantee work?

Every file processed on our servers is tagged with an automated cryptographic Time-To-Live (TTL) token expiring in 120 minutes. Upon token expiration, our automated garbage collection daemon executes an irrecoverable deletion across all memory buffers, temporary render caches, and thumbnail caches. No unencrypted backups are kept.
